1. Enter the game intentionally and select the development powers arena. Walk right to collect growth/projectile powers; verify the form/reserve readout and collision body agree. 2. Take off after a gliding run-up, steer climb/glide/dive/recovery and land without tunneling. Repeat adjacent to a thin wall, moving platform and low ceiling. Pause and resume while powered without advancing the duration or retaining held input.
3. Ride, swallow/spit red/blue/yellow shells, dismount safely, take damage and recover one fleeing mount. Feed a baby, use wings and enter a restricted destination without duplicate ownership.

5. Strike every block variant with its supported action. Activate and expire a temporary switch while occupying changed geometry; safe materialization cannot clip through a wall. Complete a sanctuary and verify its campaign flag after task-05 persistence exists.
6. Climb and flip a fence. Attack the newly selected side; the opposite side must not cause body damage. Verify distinct enemy attack responses and each of ten boss methods, including staged final encounter and same-tick terminal defeat precedence.
