# Campaign authority (task 04)

`src/core/campaign/reducer.ts` owns campaign commands. `TerrainSimulation` owns movement and emits facts; it never deducts lives or increments the campaign's reward counters. Command results are recursively frozen and carry a campaign persistence intent, not a claim that disk or cloud storage succeeded. Task 05 implements that transaction boundary; task 07 connects complete menus and the final game host.

The host supplies a UUID attempt identity and ISO save timestamp. It passes all facts from one simulation tick together to `applyEvents`; splitting a tick across calls would defeat same-tick lethal precedence. A death suppresses other facts from that tick, while earlier ticks remain valid. Rewards precede checkpoint/exit overlays, which replace stale simulation counters with current reducer coins, score and stars. Lives are always read from the current campaign and are absent from restart snapshots. Duplicate UUID facts and stale attempts do not award or deduct again. The host starts a fresh attempt after continuation or entry.

Easy/Classic/Hard start with 10/5/3 lives, timer multipliers 1.5/1/0.8 and contact protection 120/60/30 ticks. Checkpoint availability is authored by mode; the intended density is 3/2/1. Physics and terrain stay the same. Difficulty decreases only at the map; equal difficulty is a no-op and increases require a new campaign.

Game Over immediately renews lives once and commits a map continuation. Easy erases the active level's owned progress, Classic the active world's, Hard the entire campaign. Owned exits, medallions, sanctuaries and unique reward marks are revoked together. Classic retains visited world entrances, including a previously visited second secret world, but erased shortcuts and secret-network routes relock. Story victory and secret-completion cosmetics are recomputed from surviving progress. Hard keeps the campaign identity and starts at the initial entrance; another slot is untouched. Continue never awards lives again.

Coins carry over below 100; crossing 100 grants a life, maximum 99. Score saturates at 9,999,999. The airborne defeat chain awards 100, 200, 400, 800, 1,000, 2,000, 4,000, 8,000 then lives; actual landing emits `chainReset`. Medallions accumulate tentatively and commit at checkpoint or exit; one committed five-medallion set grants one life and a level-owned ledger mark. Ordinary collectible coins and life items emit one reward per attempt. Repeatable supply rooms use fresh attempts, never synthetic unique completion marks.

Engineering defaults for the original bonus game: a 3-by-3 symbol grid has one deterministic center and eight timed stops. Each row, column or diagonal of equal symbols grants one life, with saturation. An exit can award 0–99 stars; a first unique exit crossing 100 creates one bonus round, subtracts 100 and saves its continuation. Replay of a completed exit grants neither extra threshold stars nor time-conversion score. Remaining whole seconds convert to 50 score each on that first exit. These defaults introduce no wall clock or random source.

`Campaign.pendingBonus` is optional in schema v1: absent means no interrupted round. Its ID, seed, tick and stopped cells are persisted at each stop; reopen resumes the exact remaining stops and cannot duplicate line rewards. `Campaign.openingSeen` is optional in v1: absent means false. Task 07's story controller sets it after displaying the opening, so task 05's snapshot codec must preserve it. No schema migration or second save authority is required for either optional field.

`catalogIssues` verifies level/world/route/exit/sanctuary references without pretending small test arenas are the authored campaign. Production content completeness and geometry diversity are task 06. Completion always displays separate denominators of 24 levels, 34 exits and 120 medallions; aggregate percentage remains below 100 until all three are exact. The final boss ends the short story independently of full exploration.

Six-slot commands reject invalid slot numbers, occupied creation and campaign identity replacement; `replaceActiveSlot` applies a successful reducer result to exactly its campaign. Real save menus, deletion confirmation and local/account namespaces are implemented by tasks 05/07.
