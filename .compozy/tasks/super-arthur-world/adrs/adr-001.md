# ADR-001: Complete mechanical scope with original Brazilian-inspired identity

## Status
Accepted

## Date
2026-10-11

## Context
The user is testing AI programming capability through a browser platform game and Spec Driven Development. The reference is Super Mario World (SNES), but the user explicitly chose original characters, art, music and levels. Later answers renamed the title and protagonist and removed two-player play.

## Decision
Create Super Arthur World, a single-player platform game starring Arthur. Use restrained SNES-era 16-bit pixel art and fantasy inspired by Brazilian landscapes and reinterpreted creatures. Provide 10 main worlds, 2 secret worlds and 120 original levels. Preserve the reference's mechanical families, subject to recorded user overrides. Use a short story with few dialogues.

## Alternatives Considered
### Literal reproduction
Offers direct visual comparison but contradicts the selected original identity and campaign.
### Small gameplay demonstration
Would be quicker but removes the explicitly selected full mechanical scope and 120 levels.
### Two-player campaign
Was initially selected, then explicitly withdrawn by the user. It is superseded by single-player only.

## Consequences
### Positive
Clear product identity and full-scope acceptance boundary.
### Negative
Original content for 120 levels still requires meaningful level design, not cosmetic duplicates.
### Risks
The reference manual is not exhaustive. Maintain a reference inventory, source confidence and story mapping; do not claim perfect fidelity solely from reading a manual.

## Implementation Notes
Framework and code reuse belong to the Techspec. Keep the implementation simple and focused on bugs.
