# ADR-003: Six guest-capable saves with optional account synchronization

## Status
Accepted

## Date
2026-10-11

## Context
The game must support computers, phones and tablets without requiring registration. The user selected optional accounts, checkpoint autosave and explicit conflict resolution.

## Decision
Provide six independent single-player save slots. Guests save locally. Account holders may synchronize across devices. Autosave at checkpoints and level completion. Offer guest-save import; occupied-slot collisions and divergent device saves require the player's explicit choice between versions. Do not automatically merge progression.

## Alternatives Considered
### Mandatory account
Easier ownership assumptions but contradicts guest access.
### Local-only saves
Simpler but removes selected cross-device continuation.
### Automatically keep the newest save or merge unlocked progress
Convenient but rejected and potentially inconsistent with Game Over penalties.

## Consequences
### Positive
Immediate play with an optional continuation benefit.
### Negative
Conflict and import UI are necessary even for this non-commercial experiment.
### Risks
Never claim a cloud save succeeded before it did. Retain usable local state on failures and keep account and guest data isolated.

## Implementation Notes
Authentication method and synchronization contracts are Techspec decisions. Avoid enterprise account-management features.
